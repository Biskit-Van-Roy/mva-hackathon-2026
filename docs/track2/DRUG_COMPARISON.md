# Track 2 - Drug Candidate Comparison

## Objective

Compare approved aminoglycoside antibiotics
as experimental candidates for premature
termination codon readthrough.

Research only. No treatment recommendation.

## Candidate 1: Gentamicin

Mechanism hypothesis:
Premature-stop codon readthrough.

Evidence:
PMID 12072912
PMID 22479203

Evidence type:
Primary experimental, indirect for BUB1B.

BUB1B-specific efficacy:
Not demonstrated.

Limitations:
Strong sequence-context dependence.
Nephrotoxicity and ototoxicity.

## Candidate 2: Amikacin

Mechanism hypothesis:
Premature-stop codon readthrough.

Evidence:
PMID 12072912

Evidence type:
Primary experimental, indirect for BUB1B.

Study observation:
Readthrough generally comparable to
gentamicin, with context-specific differences.

BUB1B-specific efficacy:
Not demonstrated.

Limitations:
Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity and
unknown variant-specific response.

## Candidate 3: Tobramycin

Mechanism hypothesis:
Premature-stop codon readthrough.

Evidence:
PMID 12072912
PMID 14991821

Evidence type:
Primary experimental, indirect for BUB1B.

Study observation:
Lower suppression than gentamicin
in the Keeling and Bedwell comparison.

BUB1B-specific efficacy:
Not demonstrated.

Limitations:
Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity and
unknown variant-specific response.

## Interpretation

No candidate has demonstrated functional
BUBR1 rescue for the investigated variants.

Relative activity in unrelated genes
cannot establish efficacy in MVA.

Sequence-context validation and
functional rescue experiments are required.

## Research priorities

1. Validate the transcript and variant context.
2. Assess nonsense-mediated mRNA decay.
3. Evaluate sequence-specific readthrough.
4. Measure functional BUBR1 restoration.
5. Quantify chromosomal instability.
6. Evaluate toxicity and feasibility.
