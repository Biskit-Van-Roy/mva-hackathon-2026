Drug class: Aminoglycoside antibiotic
Research hypothesis: Premature-stop readthrough
Regulatory status: Approved for bacterial infections
MVA indication: Not approved

Potential relevance:
Investigate whether premature termination
readthrough could restore measurable BUBR1
protein function.
